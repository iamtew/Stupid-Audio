About Stupid Audio

Stupid Audio is a small line of free, open-source VST3 and standalone instruments. The job is not a wall of “pro” features. The job is tools that load, make a sound, and get out of the way so a track actually happens.

Each plugin does one job hard: acid lines, a kick that means it, perc that grooves, pads for the fog, and a psytrance fart designer that is not joking. They are not circuit clones and they are not a subscription. MIT. Steal them, twist them, file an issue if something breaks.

The line is separate GitHub repos, each with its own site, manual, and releases. This site is the front door.

Built by one person who wanted the boxes on the screen to match the boxes in his head. If a knob is there, it does something. If a page is empty, that part is off.
