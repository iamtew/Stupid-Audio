About Stupid Audio

Stupid Audio is a small line of free, open-source VST3 and standalone instruments. They are meant for people who already have a track in mind and need a few dedicated tools to get it down. Each plugin is built to do one job well: acid lines, kicks, percussion, pads and atmosphere, and a psytrance fart designer. Nothing here tries to be a whole studio. The idea is a focused box you can load, play, and keep using.

They are MIT licensed, so you can use them, change them, and share your own versions without a paywall or a dongle. Source, manuals, and releases live in the open so you can see how a thing works if you want to. If something breaks, or a knob does not do what you expected, open an issue and it will get looked at.

Each plugin has its own GitHub repo, with its own site, manual, and downloads. Versions move on their own schedule, so a kick update does not wait on a pad plugin, and the other way around. This site is the front door: a place to meet the line, then follow the link to the plugin you actually need.

Built by one person who wanted tools that load, make a sound, and leave room for the track. The plugins exist because those sounds were missing from the session, not because a catalog needed filling. Knobs that are on the panel do something. Pages that look quiet are just off. If it helps you finish a track, it did the job.
